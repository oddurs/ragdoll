## Problem

In `v0.1.0`, `H264Encoder` and `H264Decoder` encode/decode `I_16x16` with DC-only residuals, `P_Skip`, and `P_L0_16x16 (cbp=0)`, falling back to uncompressed `I_PCM` (`384` bytes/MB) whenever a macroblock has non-flat AC detail. Production H.264 requires full quantized $4\times4$ luma/chroma AC/DC DCT residual coding (`coded_block_pattern != 0`) across both Intra and Inter macroblocks so `I_PCM` is never triggered on natural video.

## Architecture & SIMD Design

1. Implement full `coded_block_pattern` (`cbp_luma` `0..15`, `cbp_chroma` `0..2`) encoding and decoding in `H264Encoder` and `H264Decoder` for `I_16x16` (16 luma AC $4\times4$ blocks + $2\times2$ chroma DC + 8 chroma AC $4\times4$ blocks), `I_4x4` (with `prev_intra4x4_pred_mode_flag` / `rem_intra4x4_pred_mode` and 16 luma $4\times4$ DCT blocks), `P_L0_16x16`, and `P_8x8` sub-partitions.
2. Integrate `sub4x4_dct_u8`, dead-zone trellis/scalar quantization (`quantize_4x4`, `quantize_dc_4x4`, `quantize_dc_2x2`), `dequantize_4x4`, and `add4x4_idct_u8` into the encoder reconstruction loop so `prev_recon` is bit-identical to the decoder's reconstructed reference frame.
3. Add half-pel and quarter-pel refinement (`search_qpel_refinement_16x16`) around the best integer diamond MV using `SIMD-0038`'s 6-tap FIR interpolator and `satd_4x4_u8` / `satd_8x8_u8` Hadamard cost.
4. Enable the ITU-T H.264 8.7 in-loop deblocking filter (`disable_deblocking_filter_idc = 0`) on reconstructed frames.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] `H264Encoder` encodes natural 1080p/4K video with `0%` `I_PCM` macroblocks for `QP >= 10`, achieving `>3x` smaller bitstreams than `v0.1.0` at equal or higher PSNR/SSIM.
- [ ] `H264Decoder` decodes Baseline H.264 streams generated by `ffmpeg -c:v libx264 -profile:v baseline` with `I_4x4`, `I_16x16`, `P_L0_16x16`, and `P_8x8` non-zero `cbp` residuals bit-exact to `ffmpeg`.
- [ ] Bidirectional roundtrip (`Ragdoll -> FFmpeg` and `FFmpeg -> Ragdoll`) passes across multi-GOP moving video.

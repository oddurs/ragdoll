## Problem

Codec development, perceptual quality metrics (`PSNR`/`SSIM`/`VMAF-lite`), and frame/contact-sheet extraction require fast, zero-overhead uncompressed video, image, and audio containers.

## Architecture & SIMD Design

1. **Y4M (`YUV4MPEG2`)**: Zero-copy frame slicing and muxing supporting `C420`, `C420mpeg2`, `C422`, `C444`, and 10-bit planar formats with exact `F<num>:<den>` framerates and aspect ratios.
2. **PPM / PGM**: Fast binary `P6` (RGB24) and `P5` (Luma) image readers/writers, plus multi-frame contact-sheet grid composer.
3. **RIFF WAV**: Zero-copy PCM demuxer and muxer supporting `u8`, `s16le`, `s24le`, `s32le`, and `IEEE Float f32le` (including `WAVE_FORMAT_EXTENSIBLE`).

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] Y4M, PPM/PGM, and WAV readers and writers round-trip video and audio frames bit-identically.
- [x] Files generated by Ragdoll are accepted by `ffmpeg` and `ffprobe` without warnings.

## 2026-10-24

Implemented Y4mDemuxer/Y4mMuxer (y4m.rs), Netpbm P6/P5 reader/writer + compose_contact_sheet_rgb24 (ppm.rs), and zero-copy WavDemuxer/WavMuxer supporting PCM u8/s16le/s24le/s32le and IEEE Float f32le including WAVE_FORMAT_EXTENSIBLE (wav.rs), all verified bit-exact and accepted cleanly by ffprobe/ffmpeg.

## Result

Shipped Y4M, PPM/PGM + contact-sheet composer, and RIFF WAV demuxers and muxers verified with ffprobe/ffmpeg.

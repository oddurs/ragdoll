# ragdoll-codec

Video and audio codecs, written from the specifications (the Opus codec is a port of libopus). See [Formats & codecs](/docs/formats/) for exactly which features are implemented.

[Source](https://github.com/oddurs/ragdoll/tree/main/crates/ragdoll-codec)

## H.264 / AVC (`h264`, `cabac`)

**Bitstream layer.** `parse_nal_rbsp` splits NAL units and strips emulation prevention. `Sps`, `Pps`, `SliceHeader` and `SeiMessage` parse and serialize their RBSP payloads, including VUI timing, aspect ratio, colorimetry and cropping.

**Transform and quantization.** The 4×4 and 8×8 forward and inverse integer transforms (through `ragdoll-simd`), the 4×4 luma DC and 2×2 chroma DC Hadamard, and the QP 0–51 scaling tables. `quantize_block4x4` / `quantize_block8x8` and their `dequantize_*` counterparts follow the reference rounding.

**Prediction.** Intra 16×16 (vertical, horizontal, DC, plane) and 8×8 chroma prediction here, and the nine intra 4×4 modes, quarter-pel motion compensation and the deblocking filter in `ragdoll-simd`, each with NEON and scalar implementations.

**Entropy coding.** CAVLC (`encode_cavlc_block`, `decode_cavlc_block`) and CABAC (`CabacEncoder`, `CabacDecoder`, `CabacContext`, `init_cabac_contexts`).

**Decoder.** `H264Decoder::new()` takes parameter sets with `set_extradata` and access units with `decode_annex_b_packet`, which returns frames as they leave the decoded picture buffer; `decode_stream_pts_ordered` decodes a whole Annex-B stream into presentation order. It handles Baseline, Main and High: CAVLC and CABAC, P and B slices, the 8×8 transform, weighted prediction, multiple slices, long-term references and MMCO, field pictures (PAFF), MBAFF and 4:0:0. Output is bit-exact with the JM reference decoder and FFmpeg; `tests/h264_reference.rs` checks it against hashes from both. It does not support 4:2:2, 4:4:4, bit depths above 8, lossless, SP/SI slices or slice groups. `with_threads(n)` decodes frame pictures on `n` worker threads, each waiting only for the reference rows its motion vectors reach; the output is identical to one thread's, and `flush` returns the pictures still in flight at the end of a stream.

**Encoder.** `H264Encoder::new(width, height, fps, qp)` produces Constrained Baseline (CAVLC) access units from 8-bit 4:2:0 frames. `with_profile(H264Profile::Main)` or `High` switches to CABAC, and High adds the adaptive 8×8 transform. `with_rate_control` takes a `RateControlMode`: `FixedQp`, `Crf` (with variance-adaptive quantization) or `Abr`, the last two with optional VBV `maxrate` and `bufsize`. Macroblocks are `I_4x4`, `I_16x16`, `P_L0_16x16` with quarter-pel refinement, and `P_Skip`, with the in-loop deblocking filter on. Slices are I and P only; there are no B-frames. `encode_frame` returns one Annex-B access unit per frame, with an IDR every `keyint` frames (30 by default; the CLI uses 15).

<Callout kind="status" title="Performance">
The decoder runs 1080p High-profile CABAC at about 162 fps on one thread, level with libavcodec, and at about 545 fps with four threads, ahead of libavcodec's frame threading (461 fps). The encoder makes smaller files than x264's `ultrafast` preset at equal quality and is slightly faster; `veryfast` still compresses about a quarter better. See [Benchmarks](/docs/benchmarks/).
</Callout>

## AAC-LC (`aac`)

- `AacDecoder::new(sample_rate, channels)`, or `AacDecoder::from_asc` / `with_extra_data` from an `AudioSpecificConfig`, decodes one access unit (raw, or with an ADTS header) per `decode_packet` call into 1024 samples per channel of `f32`. It covers SCE, CPE, LFE, DSE, PCE and FIL elements, M/S and intensity stereo, PNS, TNS, pulse data and every window sequence, and stays within the ISO/IEC 14496-26 conformance tolerance of FFmpeg's float decoder. Coupling channel elements are rejected.
- `AacEncoder::new(sample_rate, channels, bitrate_bps)` encodes mono or stereo with long blocks only (KBD window, 2048-point MDCT). `extra_data()` returns the two-byte `AudioSpecificConfig`; `encode_raw_frame`, `encode_adts_frame` and `encode_stream_frames` produce access units.
- `AudioSpecificConfig` and `AdtsHeader` parse and write the MP4/Matroska and ADTS headers.

<Callout kind="status" title="AAC rate control">
The encoder holds `bitrate_bps` with a bit reservoir: each frame's quantizer offset is bisected on exact bit counts, and the average bitrate lands within 5% of the target from 32 to 320 kbit/s on speech, music and synthetic signals. It uses long blocks only, and its quality has been compared by SNR, not with a psychoacoustic model.
</Callout>

## Opus (`opus`)

A port of fixed-point libopus 1.3.1, so it carries libopus's BSD-3-Clause notice (`src/opus/COPYING.libopus`).

- `OpusDecoder::new(channels).with_extra_data(&opus_head)?` decodes CELT, SILK and hybrid packets, mode switches, packet loss concealment, in-band FEC and DTX at 48 kHz, sample-exact with libopus on the RFC 8251 test vectors. `decode_packet` returns `f32`, `decode_packet_s16` and `decode_i16` 16-bit samples, and `decode_i16(None)` conceals a lost packet. `pre_skip()` is left to the caller.
- `OpusEncoder::new(sample_rate, channels, bitrate_bps)` encodes 48 kHz mono or stereo into 20 ms CELT packets, byte-identical with libopus in its restricted low-delay mode. `packet_encoder()` exposes `OpusPacketEncoder` for VBR, constrained VBR or CBR and the complexity setting.
- `OpusHead`, `OpusToc`, `build_opus_tags` and `parse_opus_tags` handle the Ogg and Matroska headers.

## Resampling and loudness (`resample`, `loudness`)

`PolyphaseResampler` converts sample rates with a Kaiser-windowed sinc filter bank and an exact rational phase accumulator; `ChannelRematrixer` mixes between mono, stereo, 5.1 and 7.1 with ITU-R BS.775 coefficients. `EbuR128Meter` measures integrated, short-term and momentary loudness, loudness range and true peak (ITU-R BS.1770-4 / EBU R128), and `normalize_audio_frame` applies a loudness target with a true-peak ceiling.

## FLAC (`flac`)

An RFC 9639 encoder and decoder for 16 and 24-bit audio.

- `FlacDecoder::decode_stream(&[u8])` returns the `FlacStreamInfo` and the decoded `AudioFrame`. Frame-level decoding into planar buffers is available for streaming use.
- `FlacEncoder::new(sample_rate, channels, bits_per_sample)` chooses per subframe between fixed predictors (orders 0–4, evaluated in one SIMD pass) and Levinson-Durbin FIR LPC (orders 1–12), Rice-codes the residual with an estimated optimal parameter, and picks independent, left/side, right/side or mid/side stereo per frame.
- Frame headers carry CRC-8 and frames carry CRC-16. `compute_flac_pcm_md5_planar` produces the `STREAMINFO` MD5; the encoder computes it only with `with_md5(true)`.

## PCM (`pcm`)

Conversion between `u8`, `s16le`, `s24le`, `s32le` and `f32le`; stereo interleaving and deinterleaving; mid/side decorrelation and reconstruction. Hot conversions have NEON kernels with scalar twins.

## RGDI: Ragdoll Fast-Intra (`rgdi`)

An intra-only codec designed for intermediate files: capture, caching and scrubbing, where decode speed matters more than size. It is not a distribution format.

- Each plane is coded with 16-pixel vertical scanline prediction, then zero-run coalescing (up to 2,048 pixels in one byte), 4-bit packed deltas, or 8-bit literal deltas.
- `RagdollIntraEncoder::new(qstep_shift)`: `0` is bit-exact lossless; `1` (what the CLI uses) or `2` quantizes the deltas. On a 720p test pattern, `1` measures about 50 dB PSNR.
- `RagdollIntraDecoder::decode_frame` reverses it; encode and decode have NEON paths.

## AV1 (`av1`)

Inspection and packaging, not decoding.

- `split_obus` and `parse_obu_header` walk OBU streams; `encode_obu` writes them.
- `Av1SequenceHeader` parses profile, level, bit depth, chroma subsampling and color description, matching `dav1d`'s parser.
- Builds `av1C` configuration records for MP4 and Matroska.
- `parse_ivf_stream` reads IVF files.

## Example: WAV to FLAC in memory

```rust
use ragdoll_codec::{FlacDecoder, FlacEncoder};

fn roundtrip(audio: &ragdoll_core::AudioFrame) -> ragdoll_core::Result<()> {
    let encoder = FlacEncoder::new(audio.sample_rate, audio.channels, 16);
    let flac = encoder.encode_stream(audio)?;

    let (info, decoded) = FlacDecoder::decode_stream(&flac)?;
    assert_eq!(decoded.num_samples, audio.num_samples);
    println!("{} Hz, {} bytes", info.sample_rate, flac.len());
    Ok(())
}
```
